## The setup

My first job out of university: junior marketing coordinator at Intersport Greece. One of my jobs was coordinating the brand's presence at the Athens Half Marathon and the Athens Marathon.

## The plot

The marathon needed a shop. The shop needed to open in five days. Five days, and every department in the company had a piece of it: their own priorities, their own deadlines and their own way of saying "that's not possible".

## The twist

It was possible. My job was to be the person who talks to all of them, translates between them and keeps the clock in everyone's head.

## The moral

Every department speaks its own language. Learn all of them, and things open on time. Turns out that's also the job description of a producer.
