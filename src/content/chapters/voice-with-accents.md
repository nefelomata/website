## The setup

At FlexCar I owned the brand voice and the copy across digital, social and offline channels.

## The plot

The voice had to travel. I worked on localized campaigns for Italy and Mexico: same brand, same personality, different accent.

## The twist

The hardest audience was inside the building. I created an employer brand (EVP) campaign and set the tone of voice for internal communication, and delivered a full 360 campaign along the way.

## The moral

A tone of voice isn't a PDF. It's every caption, every video and every email, and somebody has to care about all of them. I did, with a graphic designer at my side for the social videos and visuals.
