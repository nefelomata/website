## The setup

Copywriter at DDB Greece. The brief could be anything, and so could the format.

## The plot

Concepts and copy for TV, radio, print, out-of-home, digital and newsletters. Pitches. Brainstorms where the first ten ideas are the ones everyone else would also have, and the job is getting to the eleventh.

## The twist

I also owned a client's social media calendar from the first idea to the moment it went live. Nobody else to blame, nobody else to thank.

## The moral

Write it for fifteen seconds of TV, then for one line on a billboard. Same idea, different frame. That's very much a film lesson too.
