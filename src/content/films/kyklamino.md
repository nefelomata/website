Kyklamino was an art video shot over two days, and I was the production manager.

That meant the plan was mine: the shooting schedule, the daily call sheet, getting cast and crew to a forest at night and back again, and making sure nobody worked a night shoot on an empty stomach.

If you were on set and everything simply happened when it was supposed to, you're welcome.
