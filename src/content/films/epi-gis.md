Epi Gis (On Earth) is a short film about a woman who appears on Earth and starts to discover it. Two shoot days, and I was a production assistant.

My side of the story: catering, transport and helping keep the budget honest. Not glamorous, completely essential, and the reason the glamorous parts get to happen.
