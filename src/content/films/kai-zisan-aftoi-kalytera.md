Kai Zisan Aftoi Kalytera (And They Lived Happily Ever After) is a short film about two people with the same need, to escape their everyday lives, and one big difference: they live on two different continents.

I was the script supervisor. If a glass was half full in the wide, it was half full in the close-up. That was me.
